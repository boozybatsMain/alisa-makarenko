Approach

How I shoot

  • Noticing what others pass by

    How the light falls, where the eye lingers, what happens between the main moments. I want to catch the things other people might simply not film.

  • Details, up close

    Pine needles under snow, a cat's fur in the sun, fabric, the pages of a book. The small things others would not film are what carry the feeling of a place.

  • The light that is already there

    I work with the light I find: daylight, overcast, evening, candlelight. The edit stays simple, so the frame does the work.

  • Shot, edited and graded by me

    I shoot on iPhone, edit in DaVinci Resolve and CapCut, and do the colour myself.

Process

How it works

  1. 01

    Brief

    We talk through the place or product, the goal, the mood and where the video will be posted.

  2. 02

    Shoot

    I film on location, at a time chosen for the light and the rhythm of the place.

  3. 03

    Edit and grade

    I select the best shots, edit them and grade the colour to the look of your place or brand.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    I hand over the finished clips and, if you need them, the raw files, in formats for your platforms.
