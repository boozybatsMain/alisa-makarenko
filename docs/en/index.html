Alisa Makarenko

A one-woman production unit. For three years I've been producing Witching Hour, a YouTube channel and podcast, and running its full content production cycle: from concept, content strategy and scriptwriting to production management, post-production, release, promotion, analytics and community building.

In 2025 the project reached 30.6K listeners on Yandex Music and more than 7.4M minutes of listening. Its most popular episode was played 18.4K times that year.

Open to: YouTube Producer, Creative Producer, Content Producer

Witching Hour (Ведьмин час)

Witching Hour is an independent media project launched in December 2023. Together with my co-host I'm growing it as a multi-platform content product: we develop topics and scripts, produce conversational episodes, and grow the YouTube channel, podcast distribution and our own community on Telegram and Boosty.
