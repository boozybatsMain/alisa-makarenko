01Case

Witching Hour (Ведьмин час)

An independent YouTube show and podcast about the Harry Potter universe

Witching Hour is an independent Russian-language show I have been making with my co-host Katya since December 2023. Each episode is an hour-long, scripted conversation about a character, place or idea from the wizarding world. It goes out on YouTube, Yandex Music, Apple Podcasts and other platforms via Mave, with a community around it on Telegram and Boosty.

What I did and what came of it

Shorts

Two quick questions: more than two thirds of all channel views

In June and July 2024 my co-host and I released 11 Shorts, most of them built around one quick question about the books. The one on why Lupin took the train to Hogwarts (30 seconds) reached 1.76M views; the one on how the films treated Ron Weasley (19 seconds) reached 1.57M.

The channel's 13 Shorts total 4.55M views, about 92% of all its YouTube views.

Comeback

Back on YouTube after seven months

After a break from long-form YouTube episodes since April 2026, we came back on Nov 5 with an hour-long Halloween episode, Vampires vs Werewolves. In its first 14 days it reached 8.2K views, ranking first of the channel's last 10 videos over the same period. On Apple Podcasts, 200 people listened in the first 15 days, 77% more than the show's median episode by the same day after release.

  • +53%watch time, above the channel's usual range
  • +45%subscriber growth (84 net new), also above the usual range

Whole channel: last 28 days (Oct 23 – Nov 19, 2026, incl. the Nov 5 release) vs the previous 28 days

Note. No new videos came out in the previous 28 days. Channel views rose 65% but stayed within the channel's usual range (13.8K–30.5K per 28 days); only watch time and subscriber growth went above it.

Source: YouTube Studio, Oct 23 – Nov 19, 2026; Apple Podcasts Connect

02Process

The full cycle, from idea to release

This is how every Witching Hour episode gets made. I run most of the stages myself.

  1. 01Idea
  2. 02Research
  3. 03Concept and script
  4. 04Pre-production
  5. 05People and workflow
  6. 06Recording
  7. 07Editing and sound
  8. 08Release and promotion
08 / 08
  1. 01

    Idea

    I pick a topic and an angle: a character, place or piece of news that can carry an hour of conversation.

    Tools: NotionChatGPTClaude

  2. 02

    Research

    I gather material from the books, films and other sources and check the facts.

    Tools: PerplexityChatGPTNotion

  3. 03

    Concept and script

    I write the script to a fixed structure: greeting, intro, a Boosty and Telegram plug, four to six themed segments, sign-off.

    Tools: Notion

  4. 04

    Pre-production

    I plan the session and prepare lights, microphones and the audio interface.

    Tools: Focusrite Scarlett Solo

  5. 05

    People and workflow

    I align schedules with my co-host and guests and keep tasks, templates and the episode archive in one workspace.

    Tools: NotionTelegram

  6. 06

    Recording

    I record the episode on camera as co-host while keeping an eye on picture and sound.

    Tools: iPhoneOBS Studio

  7. 07

    Editing and sound

    I clean up and mix the audio, edit the video together with my co-host, and make the Shorts.

    Tools: REAPERStudio OneDaVinci ResolveCapCut

  8. 08

    Release and promotion

    I make the thumbnail, title, description and chapters, publish to YouTube and podcast platforms, announce on Telegram and review the analytics.

    Tools: PhotoshopYouTube StudioMaveTelegramBoosty

04Videography

Quiet footage: nature and still life

Outside the show I shoot short, quiet videos: mountains, water, animals, objects and small everyday details. I shoot, edit and color-grade them myself. I shoot and deliver finished footage that a guest house, hotel or local business can use however it needs.

What I can deliver

  • Vertical clips for Reels and Shorts
  • Atmospheric footage of a place and its details
  • Product and still-life video
  • Editing and color grading to a brand's look
  • UGC videos
